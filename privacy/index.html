Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information NorthPaw collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. NorthPaw is a project by Next Steps Beyond.

Effective date: April 11, 2026

1. What NorthPaw collects

NorthPaw may collect the following information when you visit the site, join the waitlist, or respond to a survey:

  • Email address
  • Optional first name
  • Survey responses and product feedback
  • Basic analytics and site usage information
  • Information you choose to send directly by email

Waitlist information

If you join the NorthPaw early access list, your email address and any optional name information you provide may be stored for product updates, early access invitations, and launch communication.

Survey information

If you complete the NorthPaw survey, your responses may be used to understand what features, workflows, and use cases are most useful to potential users.

2. How information is used

NorthPaw uses collected information to:

  • Manage the early access and waitlist process
  • Send launch updates, product previews, and testing invitations
  • Understand user needs and prioritize product development
  • Improve the website, messaging, and product direction
  • Monitor basic site performance and traffic trends
  • Respond to questions, requests, or feedback

3. Third-party services

NorthPaw uses third-party services to operate parts of the website and early access flow. These providers may process information according to their own privacy practices.

  • MailerLite for waitlist and email updates
  • Tally for surveys and feedback collection
  • Cloudflare for website hosting, security, and analytics-related infrastructure

NorthPaw may update this list over time if tools or providers change.

4. Analytics and cookies

NorthPaw may use privacy-conscious analytics and similar technologies to understand website traffic, site performance, and general usage patterns. This information is used to improve the site and understand whether the product and messaging are resonating.

NorthPaw is not intended to sell personal information or use invasive tracking for the purpose of third-party advertising resale.

5. Email communications

If you join the waitlist, NorthPaw may email you about launch updates, early testing, product previews, or related product communication. You can unsubscribe from those emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in the email, when available, or by contacting the email address listed below.

6. Data retention

Information may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the waitlist, review product feedback, communicate about early access, comply with legal obligations, or protect the integrity of the site and service.

7. Your choices

You may request to:

  • Opt out of future email communications
  • Request deletion of your waitlist information
  • Ask what information you have submitted directly to NorthPaw

To make one of these requests, use the contact email below.

8. Children’s privacy

NorthPaw is not intended for children under 13, and the site is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as NorthPaw evolves. If the policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, opt-out requests, or deletion requests, contact:
