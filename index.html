What NorthPaw focuses on

Pavement safety, translated into action

NorthPaw estimates surface temperatures using local air temperature, wind speed, relative humidity, and solar load from sky cover.

Thermodynamic timeline

Compare asphalt, concrete, sand, and artificial turf risk windows through the day.

Canine-weighted NPI

Flat-faced, thick-coated, and high-energy dogs receive earlier warnings when conditions demand it.

Dynamic checklists

Rain can trigger paw towels, high NPI can flag extra water, and sand or turf can change paw protection notes.

Release focus

Built for the small decisions that make outdoor dog life safer.

Surface timeline

A plain-language read on when each selected surface may be comfortable, cautionary, or unsafe.

Profiles & pack lists

Checklist suggestions adjust to weather, selected surfaces, and your dog’s cooling and fatigue profile.

Offline field guide

Built for real dog outings

  • Neighborhood walks across heat-holding pavement

  • Sports fields and artificial turf that can spike under sun

NorthPaw is for people who want practical safety context without accounts, login walls, or background location tracking.

Why NorthPaw feels different

Weather apps
